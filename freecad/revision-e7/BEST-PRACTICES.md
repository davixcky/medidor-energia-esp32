# E.9 mechanical and electrical-design practices

These rules belong to the energy meter. They are tracked separately from the equalizer because this device contains a mains-voltage compartment.

## Sources of truth

1. KiCad defines the electrical circuit, but the current T1 sheet is explicitly stale: it still shows 5 V input and an MT3608.
2. `medidas-componentes.json` records dimensions and evidence quality.
3. `generar_modelo.py` produces CAD, STL, assembly metadata and geometry validation.
4. The PNG stills generated by Blender are the current visual reference. Old videos are not authoritative.
5. Bambu projects derive from STL and never replace slicer review or physical fit tests.

## Touch-first interface

- The LilyGO T4-S3 is horizontal and flush with the lid.
- SW1, SW2 and ENC1 are DNP. There are no physical user buttons or push encoder.
- Touch targets must be at least 9 mm, show pressed state and remain usable with measurement values changing.
- Alarm acknowledgement and data reset are separate actions; reset requires confirmation.
- Loss of ESP-NOW data, stale data and power faults are visible states, not silent failures.

## Power

- Input is 12 V DC, center-positive, through the verified CON-156A 5.5 × 2.5 mm panel connector.
- The 12 V FCOB uses the protected 12 V branch.
- A separately mounted LM2596 produces 5.0 V for logic and analog circuitry.
- Use keyed, removable two-pole harnesses from T1 protected 12 V to LM2596 input and from LM2596 5 V output back to T1. Do not distribute loads directly from loose wires at the barrel jack.
- The modeled 43 × 21 × 14 mm LM2596 envelope is conservative. The final module must be measured and tested in its coupon.
- Adjust and verify 5.0 V before connecting ESP32 or LilyGO. Validate polarity, current margin, ripple and temperature under worst-case load.

## No-glue, no-enclosure-screw assembly

- Lid: press-fit magnets and printed alignment stops.
- LilyGO: open sliding backplate with keyhole snap features; no load on central components or connectors.
- FCOB: captive printed tray; the strip’s adhesive is not required.
- Piezo: cup plus bayonet retainer.
- Perfboards: printed standoffs and elastic locating pins.
- Z1 guard: opaque cover with blind snap sockets and deliberate tool-assisted removal. This is a design feature, not evidence of safety certification.
- Connector nuts, terminal screws and adjustment trimmers intrinsic to purchased components remain allowed; they are not enclosure fasteners.

## Shared product language

- The equalizer and energy meter use the same dark body, continuous graphite lid, rounded perimeter, flush horizontal LilyGO glass, hexagonal light diffuser and parallel ventilation slots.
- Functional differences remain honest: the meter has one status bar and a segregated mains guard; it does not imitate the equalizer's five knobs or three audio-band bars.
- Safety color is restricted to the embossed warning symbol. It is an AMS/slicer paint cue on the single-piece guard, not a glued decoration.

## Fit and tolerances

- Print all coupons using the final material, nozzle and profile before printing the enclosure.
- Start with 0.20 mm clearance per side for rigid sliding fits, 0.15 mm per side for calibrated snap fits and 0.30 mm per side around purchased connector bodies. Tune from coupons.
- A published or nominal dimension is never promoted to “measured” until checked on the received part with calipers.
- Verify the actual LM2596, DC plug overmold, 3.5 mm jacks, LilyGO glass/PCB stack, LED body, piezo rim and magnets.
- Do not expose a recessed board-mounted USB-C through an oversized wall opening. Keep it internal under the removable lid, or model a specific measured snap-in panel extension.
- “Perfect fit” means the accepted coupon dimensions have been fed back into CAD; a render cannot prove it.

## Safety and render contract

- Z1 remains physically segregated and under an independent opaque guard.
- Geometry validation does not prove creepage, clearance, insulation class, fire performance, thermal margin or regulatory compliance.
- Regenerate and inspect all still images after a visible change; do not spend time rendering videos for this revision.
